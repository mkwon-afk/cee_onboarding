Escalation Management 🔗

Critical Accounts Program (CAP) — Experience Engineering
✏️ Working Document + Add link to working doc
The Critical Accounts Program (CAP) is part of Experience Engineering (XE), not CEE — but its escalation workflow is the primary path when CEE cases need heightened attention. Every CEE associate should know when and how to trigger it.

What is an escalation?

A request for heightened awareness or assistance on a difficult case from a customer with concerns centred around their experience. These are often business-critical and need faster resolution. Escalation management coordinates the right resources and verifies problems are addressed at the correct level.

Escalation lifecycle

1
Before kickoff
Background research · Business impact · Diligence on the escalation
›
2
Kickoff
Build action plan · Align teams · Exec comms · Define success criteria
›
3
Ongoing engagement
Execute plan · Manage comms · RCA progress · Adjust course as needed
›
4
Closure
Exit criteria met · Final RCA · Trigger Closed Loop program

Before triggering an escalation

1 · Open case
A support case must be open before an escalation can be requested.
2 · Severity set
Severity must reflect actual business impact. Overstating severity does not yield better results.
3 · Problem defined
Clear problem statement, business impact, and affected environments must be documented.

Paths to escalate

For Red Hatters (internal)

ACE — Active Customer Escalation tool

Slack — #escalation

Shadowbot — internal escalation bot

For customers

RME — Click "Request an escalation" in the support case (Request Management Escalation)

Escalation Hotline — Severity 1, Premium subscription only
+1 919-890-8001 (24×7)

Engage your TAM, Customer Success Executive, or Sales team as an internal advocate.

CAP approach: Reactive → Proactive → Preemptive

Reactive
Global team specialising in escalation handling and critical situation management — 24×5 availability.
Proactive
CPEC reviews customer engagement with Red Hat and spots opportunities to reduce escalation and case volume.
Preemptive
Phased Gates TSR uses AI to inspect OpenShift clusters and identify misconfigurations before they cause issues.
Closed Loop: After every escalation, CAP runs a retrospective to identify root causes and drive systemic improvements across Red Hat — preventing the same issue from recurring.
